# **Profoundness in Soundtracks**
## **Written by : Lorena363949 **

  "I've always been fond of soundtracks, for most of them invoke a raw emotion that I simply cannot find in vocal music.
I am unable to articulate my thoughts for these emotions are fleeting and are never present for longer than it needs to be,
but- I find myself mesmerized by these tracks as if I was being sucked inside the world it was created for. Some pieces somehow
convery such a dreadful situation unfolding in such a whimsical manner- while some are the opposite, converying hope but paced at
such a manner that gives the impression that it can be snuffed out at one misstep. I am unsure of how these feelings came to be
but they're so compelling that even I find myself weeping- or laughing in their place."
